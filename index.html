OpenSolar Smart Microgrid

Open Source · Charity · Electricity for All

OpenSolar.cc is an independent charity project bringing low-cost, easy-to-maintain, affordable power systems to over 1 billion people living without electricity worldwide.

Solar Power

Harnessing solar, wind, and hydro energy to provide clean electricity tailored to each off-grid community's needs.

Low-Voltage DC

Using low-voltage direct current (LVDC) technology to reduce transmission loss and improve safety and reliability.

Charity Donation

Delivering solar equipment to those who need it most through donations and volunteer efforts. First shipment to Rwanda in 2026.

Open Source

All system designs are fully open source. Anyone can contribute, localize production, and deploy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is OpenSolar.cc a registered charity project?

Yes. OpenSolar.cc is an independent solar charity project founded by Sherry Liao in 2024, dedicated to providing affordable solar power systems to energy-deprived regions worldwide.

Is OpenSolar.cc affiliated with opensolar.com?

OpenSolar.cc is a completely independent charity project with no affiliation to opensolar.com. They are two wholly separate entities. Our project focuses on solar charity donations for off-grid communities in Africa.

When did the first shipment depart for Rwanda?

50 solar home systems departed Shenzhen on June 11, 2026, expected to arrive in Kigali, Rwanda around July 5, 2026.

How can individuals or companies participate in the next donation round?

You can get involved by: (1) donating functional used solar equipment; (2) contributing via our Open Collective page; (3) volunteering to help promote the project. Contact us at news@opensolar.cc.
