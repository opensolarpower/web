About OpenSolar.cc

An independent solar charity project · Not affiliated with opensolar.com

Our History

OpenSolar.cc was founded by Sherry Liao in 2024 as an independent solar charity project.

Core values: charity, open systems, open source architecture.

Our Mission

Make electricity accessible and beneficial to all of humanity.

Our goal is to build low-cost, easy-to-maintain, affordable power systems for over 1 billion low-income people living without electricity worldwide.

Electricity has been invented for over 100 years. It should improve the quality of life for all people, just like clean air and warm sunshine.

Our Values

  • Transparency — All project progress and fund usage are publicly available
  • Open Source — All technical designs are fully open for anyone to contribute
  • Sustainability — Focus on local production and local maintenance capacity building
  • Independence — OpenSolar.cc is an independent project with no commercial affiliations
